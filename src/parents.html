Penguin Quest: parent guide

Version {{VERSION}}. Installing on a Chromebook, setting up, backups and what to expect.

What it is

A 3D adventure where a penguin explores islands, helps friends and solves math along the way. There are three books: Aurora Rescue (grade 4), The Ember Isles (grade 5) and Skyreach (grade 6). Each book has a story told in a prologue and seven chapters, about 200 math problems woven into puzzles and mini-games, and short lessons that teach a new idea the first time it comes up. Problems get harder when he is doing well, and easier, with hints, when he is stuck.

There are no ads, accounts, purchases or chat, and the game sends nothing over the internet.

1Install it on the Chromebook

  1. Open Chrome and go to jesteele79.github.io/penguin-quest. The first time needs the internet; after that the game also works offline.
  2. When the title screen appears, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar. If it is not there, open Chrome's ⋮ menu and choose Install Penguin Quest (in newer versions of Chrome it is under Cast, save and share).
  3. The game opens in its own window and appears in the Launcher. To pin it to the shelf, right-click its icon and choose Pin.

A school-managed Chromebook may not allow installing apps or opening the site. If so, ask the school, or play it in a normal Chrome tab.

2Set it up before handing it over

  1. Optional quick test. Play for a few minutes. Movement should be smooth and you should hear music. If it stutters, see "Troubleshooting" below.
  2. Start him fresh. If you played, open For grown-ups (bottom left of the title screen) and choose Reset all progress. Progress from versions before 1.0 is cleared automatically.
  3. Let him make his penguin: a name, his real school grade (this sets the difficulty) and a scarf.
  4. Choose your settings in For grown-ups:
    • Story pace. "One chapter per day" (the default) opens the next chapter the morning after he finishes one. Side quests, the daily patrol board and practice are always open. "No limit" lets him play straight through.
    • Grown-up lock. Set a four-digit PIN so only you can open For grown-ups or start a new adventure, which would erase his progress. Write the PIN down.
    • Save a first backup (see section 4).

3What to expect

  • The next book opens when he has finished the story and mastered 80% of that grade's skills. Strong students usually get there as the story ends. Most children need some extra practice first: the game shows how many skills are left and points him to a crystal where practice serves exactly those skills. You can also open the next book early in For grown-ups, under Books.
  • If he is already past a grade, open the next book early. He can then switch books from the bookshelf on the title screen, or, after the prologue, ask the captain at the dock to sail him there.
  • Help built in: a hint button on every puzzle, step-by-step explanations after a few misses, and the lessons, which he can replay from the journal. In Settings: read-aloud for questions and story, larger text, easy-reading spacing, and Gentle mode (battle creatures wait instead of advancing).
  • Lost or stuck? The pause menu has Back to the start. The gold sparkle trail always leads to the next goal.

4Backups: please read

His progress lives only in Chrome on that Chromebook. It is erased by clearing browsing data with "Cookies and other site data" ticked, by uninstalling the app, by removing his account from the Chromebook, or by a Powerwash. It does not follow him to another computer.

  • Save a backup: For grown-ups → Save a backup. A file called penguin-quest-backup-date.json lands in the Downloads folder. Do this every week or two and copy the file to Google Drive, since the Downloads folder itself is not backed up.
  • Restore: For grown-ups → Load a backup, then pick the file. It replaces the progress on that device (all three books).
  • New Chromebook: save a backup on the old one, install the game on the new one, then load the backup.

Troubleshooting

  • Choppy or slow: Settings → Graphics → Low. (Auto lowers it by itself after a few seconds of slow play, but Low is the smoothest.) Close other tabs, and plug in the charger: many Chromebooks slow down on battery.
  • No sound: check the Chromebook's volume and Settings → Music and Sound effects. Sound starts after the first click or key press.
  • Something looks wrong: close the game window and open it again. Progress is saved as he plays.
  • Updates: when a new version comes out, the installed game picks it up by itself the next time it is opened with internet (sometimes on the second opening). The version number is at the bottom of For grown-ups.
  • Forgot the PIN: in the game window press Ctrl + Shift + J, type localStorage.removeItem('penguinquest.lock') and press Enter, then close and reopen the game. This removes only the lock; progress stays. (If the Chromebook blocks that panel, the PIN cannot be recovered, so keep it written down.)
